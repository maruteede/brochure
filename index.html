Lifecycle

Supporting the full product lifecycle

A single integrated team covers every phase, from first concept to in-service support.
01

Ideation

Concept & feasibility

Concept sketches with industrial designers; feasibility, benchmarking and teardown; design-to-cost targeting; specification capture agreed with the programme team.

02

Design

Modelling & definition

3D CAD modelling, GD&T and tolerance stack-up, industrial and packaging design, DFM/DFA.

03

Engineering

Analysis, systems & validation

FEA (structural, fatigue), CFD (flow, thermal), embedded and EE architecture, wiring-harness engineering; standards and regulatory support, design validation and DVP, test plans, documentation and traceability.

04

Manufacturing

Industrialise (partners)

Manufacturing engineering, fixture and tooling design, process documentation, delivered with a vendor and partner network.

05

Sustenance

Support through life

Engineering change management, obsolescence management, field-issue root-cause analysis, continuous cost-down.

Manufacturing phase: Marutee provides the engineering; production is executed through partners and is labelled as such.
